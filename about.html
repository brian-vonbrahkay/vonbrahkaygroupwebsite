About VonBrahkay Group

Built on a "now or never" decision, and one idea about partnership.

The founder's story

VonBrahkay Group started after its founder was let go from a prior role. Rather than treating it as a setback, he treated it as a "now or never" moment — a chance to build the independent practice he had spent years helping other partners build for themselves.

What success looks like isn't a revenue target first — it's freedom: control of his time, presence with his family, and work that can't be reduced to a replaceable line item on someone else's org chart.

Empathy-driven discovery

The core of how we work is what the founder calls empathy-driven discovery — getting on the same side of the table as the client, so an engagement becomes "us and you versus the problem," not vendor versus customer.

In practice, that means we ask about your business before we ask about your network, we tell you plainly what we find even when it's not flattering, and we never let a commission-based recommendation shape an assessment finding.

It's us and you versus the problem — not vendor versus customer. — Founder, VonBrahkay Group

Most firms in this category brand around precision, vigilance, or fear of the next breach. We think the more useful thing to offer a small or mid-market organization is a partner who has genuinely thought about the problem from your side of the table — not just ours.

Where the name comes from

"VonBrahkay" is drawn from the founder's own family history — the likely pre-Anglicized German form of his actual surname, Bonebrake. "Von Brake" is a documented German surname rooted in the word for marsh or wetland.

We kept the name intentionally independent of words like "security," "cyber," or "risk." The work speaks for itself; the name is meant to be personal and lasting rather than descriptive of any one service line.
