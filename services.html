Services & pricing

Straightforward engagements, priced up front.

Every number below is a starting point for a typical engagement of that type. Your actual scope and quote depend on the size and complexity of your environment — we'll confirm exact pricing before any work begins.

1 · Flagship offering

Cybersecurity gap assessment

A point-in-time evaluation of your current security posture against an industry-standard framework, delivered as a fixed-fee engagement. You get an executive summary, control-by-control findings, and a prioritized remediation roadmap.

Tier What's included Investment Timeline
IG1 Baseline Assessment Evaluation against CIS Controls v8.1, Implementation Group 1 (56 safeguards) — foundational cyber hygiene, plus an executive summary and prioritized roadmap. Starting at $6,000 2–4 weeks
IG2 Extended Assessment Everything in IG1, plus the additional safeguards (130 total) for organizations with greater operational complexity, regulatory exposure, or sensitive data handling. Starting at $11,000 3–5 weeks

CIS Controls v8.1 is the primary framework we assess against; we reference NIST CSF and NIST 800-171 where relevant to your industry or contractual requirements.

2 · Ongoing

Ongoing security advisory

For clients who want continued accountability and progress tracking after the initial assessment.

Tier What's included Investment Cadence
Security Advisory Retainer Quarterly posture review against your assessment baseline, a living risk register, prioritized roadmap updates, and support with vendor security questionnaires. $2,000–$2,500 / month Quarterly touchbase
vCISO Program (coming soon) Hands-on security leadership: board-ready reporting, policy ownership, incident response planning, and deeper program governance. Scope defined per client. By engagement Monthly
3 · Disclosed, optional

Technology sourcing & channel advisory

Where an assessment identifies a gap, we can help you evaluate and connect with vetted providers — MDR/SOC, backup and recovery, email security, identity and access management, and related solutions — through our technology advisory network.

Full disclosure: we may be compensated by technology suppliers when you choose to procure through our advisory network. This is paid by the supplier, not billed to you, and it never changes the objectivity of an assessment finding. There is no obligation to purchase through us as a condition of any engagement.
