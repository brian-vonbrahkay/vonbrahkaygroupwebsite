How an engagement works

Four steps, from first conversation to an ongoing plan you can act on.

Discovery conversation

We start by understanding your business, not just your network — what you're protecting and why it matters.

Framework assessment

We evaluate your environment against CIS Controls v8.1 (and NIST, where relevant) — independent of anything we might later recommend.

Prioritized roadmap

You get a clear, ranked list of what to fix first and why — in plain language, not a wall of findings.

Ongoing advisory

If you want continued accountability, we check in quarterly to track progress and keep the roadmap current.
